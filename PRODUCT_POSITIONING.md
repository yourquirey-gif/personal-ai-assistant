# Personal AI — MVP Positioning

## Working brand

**Personal AI**

Product descriptor: **Personal AI Assistant**

Working tagline: **Research. Understand. Compare. Get things done.**

This is the MVP working brand. Trademark/domain availability and final legal brand clearance are not established by this document.

## Initial audience

The MVP intentionally serves a broad set of individual users rather than locking into one narrow vertical:

- Students and learners
- Job seekers and career researchers
- People doing general web research
- Freelancers and independent professionals
- People organizing everyday plans and decisions

The product should remain simple enough that a first-time user can understand the value without knowing how AI agents work.

## Core promise

**Ask → Research → Understand → Compare → Help**

The assistant should feel like one focused workspace for questions that benefit from live research, explanation, comparison, or planning.

## Brand direction

- Calm, focused, trustworthy, and practical
- Dark, minimal interface with restrained blue/lavender accents
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as “perfect answers” or “fully autonomous”
- Emphasize useful work over AI novelty
- Clearly distinguish live Google Search sources from model-generated explanation

## MVP message

> Your AI assistant for real work.

Research ideas, understand complex topics, compare options, and turn questions into clear next steps.

## What the MVP does not promise

The MVP does not claim to autonomously send emails, book appointments, control a browser, make purchases, execute code, or take other consequential external actions.
